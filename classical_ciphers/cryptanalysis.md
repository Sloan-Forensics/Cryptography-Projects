# Frequency Analysis Attack

## Ciphertext
HXD QJEN SDBC LAJLTNM CQN BNLANC VNBBJPN

## Step 1: Identify Patterns
- Repeated 3-letter word: CQN
- Likely corresponds to common word "THE"

## Step 2: Determine Shift
C → T = shift of -9  
Q → H = shift of -9  
N → E = shift of -9  

## Step 3: Apply Shift
Apply shift of -9 to entire ciphertext

## Result
YOU HAVE JUST CRACKED THE MESSAGE

## Security Insight
Frequency analysis works because:
- Letter frequency is preserved in substitution ciphers
- Common words like "THE" are predictable
- Makes classical ciphers insecure
